Debashree's Coffee Shop,Bhubaneswar.

Cake

Cake symbolizes joy, love, appreciation, and even accomplishments. It is served during weddings, birthdays, and many different party settings. The cakes are almost always made to honor or mean something to the recipient. They are edible art, to be viewed and consumed with love and happiness on the most joyous occasions. Most cakes for these types of special occasions are ordered in abakery.
